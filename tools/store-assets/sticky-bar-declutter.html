What's actually changed (proposed):
  • Phase-sub line dropped on Phase 1 — “Pre-sedation clearance” restates “Phase 1 · Assessment”. On Phase 2/3/4 the sub is kept until the bar fills with status (vitals, alerts).
  • Clearance is the bar + count only — drops the literal word “Clearance”; the bar carries the meaning. When complete, the bar tints green and the count flips to “Ready · 14 / 14”, so the separate “✓ Ready” pill goes away.
  • Saved becomes an 8 px green dot with the timestamp on its title attribute. During the < 800 ms “Saving…” window the dot animates yellow so the feedback isn't lost.
  • Progress rail kept but slimmed — 1 px hairline, no head dot. Still answers “how far through am I?” glanceably.
  • Phase label, Undo, Emergency, alert pills — all unchanged. They were earning their space.
